# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This is a personal, single-user application ("the App") built to let its
sole developer and user access their own Oura Ring data through a local
MCP (Model Context Protocol) server.

## What data the App accesses

The App connects to the Oura API (v2) via OAuth2 and may read the
following data belonging to the account holder who authorizes it:

- Personal profile information (age, weight, height, biological sex)
- Sleep, readiness, and daily activity summaries
- Heart rate data
- Workouts
- Tags logged in the Oura app

## How the data is used

All data is used exclusively by the account holder, locally, to answer
their own questions about their own health and fitness data. Data is
processed on the account holder's own machine and is not transmitted to,
stored on, or shared with any third-party server, service, or person.

## Data storage

OAuth2 access and refresh tokens are stored locally, on the account
holder's own device, in a file that is not transmitted anywhere. No Oura
data or account credentials are retained by the developer of the App.

## Third parties

The App does not share any data with third parties. It communicates only
with Oura's own API.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the contact email provided
in the App's Oura developer registration.
